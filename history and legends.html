THE STORY OF FORMULA 1

The Formula 1 World Championship began in 1950. Since then, the sport has developed from early Grand Prix racing into one of the world's most advanced forms of motorsport.

F1 TIMELINE

1950

The World Championship Begins

The first official Formula 1 World Championship season takes place.

1960s

A New Era of Engineering

Teams experiment with new engine layouts, aerodynamics and lightweight construction.

1970s

Aerodynamics Become Crucial

Ground-effect ideas and aerodynamic development transform the performance of F1 cars.

1980s

Turbo Power

Turbocharged engines become a major part of the technological battle between teams.

1990s

Technology Accelerates

Electronics, advanced aerodynamics and computer technology become increasingly important.

2000s

Global Expansion

Formula 1 continues expanding its global audience while new circuits join the calendar.

2010s

The Hybrid Era

Hybrid power units combine combustion engines with sophisticated energy recovery technology.

2020s

A New Generation

New aerodynamic regulations and continued technological development create another major chapter in F1 history.

F1 LEGENDS

Drivers who left a lasting mark on Formula 1.

01

Juan Manuel Fangio

🇦🇷 Argentina

One of the dominant drivers of the early years of the Formula 1 World Championship.

02

Ayrton Senna

🇧🇷 Brazil

Famous for his qualifying speed, wet-weather ability and intense racing style.

03

Alain Prost

🇫🇷 France

Known for his precise driving and strategic approach to racing.

04

Michael Schumacher

🇩🇪 Germany

A seven-time World Champion who became one of the most successful drivers in F1.

05

Sebastian Vettel

🇩🇪 Germany

Four-time World Champion known for his success during the early 2010s.

06

Fernando Alonso

🇪🇸 Spain

A two-time World Champion renowned for his racecraft and adaptability.

07

Lewis Hamilton

🇬🇧 United Kingdom

A seven-time World Champion and one of the most successful drivers in F1 history.
