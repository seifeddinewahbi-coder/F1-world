F1 TECHNOLOGY

Discover the technology and engineering behind a modern Formula 1 car.

⚡

Power Unit

A modern F1 power unit combines a turbocharged internal combustion engine with electrical energy systems.

🌬️

Aerodynamics

Wings, floors and diffusers control airflow around the car to create downforce and reduce drag.

🔋

Hybrid System

Electrical systems recover and store energy that can be used to improve the car's performance.

🛑

Brakes

F1 cars use advanced carbon brake systems designed to operate at extremely high temperatures.

⚙️

Suspension

The suspension keeps the tyres in contact with the track while helping control the car's handling.

🎮

Steering Wheel

The steering wheel contains controls for communication, settings, energy management and car functions.

🛞

Tyres

F1 tyres are specially designed to provide different levels of grip, performance and durability.

🛡️

Safety

The survival cell, halo, crash structures and other systems are designed to protect the driver.

HOW DOES AN F1 CAR WORK?

Every part of an F1 car has a purpose. Engineers work to balance speed, aerodynamic performance, energy efficiency, tyre performance and safety.
